Privacy Policy – Amazon Ads Reporting CLI

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This application is an internal, read-only tool used to retrieve and analyze advertising data from our own Amazon Ads account.

The application may access campaign information and advertising performance data, including impressions, clicks, advertising spend, attributed orders, and attributed sales.

The application does not create, modify, pause, or delete campaigns, bids, budgets, keywords, targets, or other advertising settings.

Authentication credentials and tokens are stored securely and are not intentionally included in application logs or shared with third parties.

Advertising data is used solely for internal reporting, analysis, and business operations. We do not sell advertising data or provide it to third parties for marketing purposes.

Access to Amazon Ads may be revoked at any time. Locally stored data may also be deleted when it is no longer required.
